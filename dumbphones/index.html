Evidence confidence

Confidence is intentionally separate from the numerical fit score. A pre-production device can be an excellent theoretical fit while still having low evidence confidence.

HighCarrier-sold or unusually strong real-world evidence.
MediumProduct is established, but important Canadian or software questions remain.
LowPre-production, unpublished critical details, contradictory seller information, or little real-world evidence.

Band support and advertised VoLTE do not guarantee Bell, TELUS/Koodo, Rogers/Fido or Freedom will provision voice service. Carrier certification, IMS configuration, firmware and IMEI policies can matter.
